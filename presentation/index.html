Before you go on stage

  1. Open the deck in Chrome and press F for fullscreen on the projector screen.
  2. Press P to open the presenter window and drag it to your laptop screen.
  3. Press → once you start talking (after the countdown it plays by itself). Press S in the deck for settings.
  4. Run Practice at least twice, then adjust the pace with + −.
